THE HOLLOW PULSE

64-second, 6/8 gothic/doom arrangement in B minor.
Plays throughout the Architect boss encounter, including the Corrupted Player
phase, using a whole-file, gapless Ogg Vorbis decoder loop.

This arrangement develops short melodic contours traced from the user-supplied
"SLAY THE SPIRE - THE HEART - SYMPHONIC METAL COVER" recording, whose artist
metadata identifies JakeWoodwardMusic:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ESQN5YV1--E
The source recording itself is not sampled or included.
Source-music rights remain with their respective creators; inclusion here does
not establish permission to distribute the underlying composition publicly.

Virtual instruments: GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins.
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser
See GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt for the complete license and sample-provenance
disclosure. The instrument bank is not bundled.

Rendered with TinySoundFont (MIT), NumPy, and FFmpeg. The arrangement uses
cello, piano, tubular bells, distorted guitars, bass, strings, and percussion.
It is a sample-based render, not a live band recording.

Format: stereo Ogg Vorbis, 44,100 Hz.
Loop start: sample 0. Loop end: sample 2,822,400 (exclusive), 64 seconds.
Instrument releases and ambience were wrapped during rendering, not faded out
or appended as silence. Keep the entire file when replacing/re-encoding it.
