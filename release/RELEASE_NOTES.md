# Act 4: Echos of the Past 1.0.1

**Your past is the final boss.**

The Architect mod is now **Act 4: Echos of the Past**, matching its Steam Workshop
title. The in-game mod name, assembly display metadata, mod-browser image and
promotional artwork use the new branding.

This is a naming and artwork update, not a gameplay or save-format change.
The internal ID, runtime filenames and installation folder remain `TheArchitect`.
Update the existing installation; do not install this as a second mod.
The Workshop update preserves the description and visibility edited on Steam.

Continue beyond Act 3 into a new fourth act for Slay the Spire 2. Meet The Unwritten,
choose an Ancient gift, prepare for the climb, and confront the Architect.
On your next visit, the deck you left behind becomes the enemy.

- A fixed Ancient, Rest Site, Shop and boss route.
- The Unwritten and eight custom Ancient relics.
- Corrupted Players using their saved decks, upgrades, enchantments and native
  card mechanics, with upcoming hands visible during your turn.
- Single-player and 2-4 player co-op, with separate host-owned saved-party lineages.
- Bound Echo corruption visuals, enemy orbs and pets, and a continuous handoff
  from the Corrupted Players to the Architect.
- Saved-deck browsing on character select and a custom ending sequence.

**Compatibility:** public-beta **v0.111.0**, BaseLib **3.4.5**.
All co-op participants need matching versions. Disable other fourth-act mods.
BaseLib must be installed separately.

**Known limitations:** co-op-only and third-party card/modifier effects on
Corrupted Players are Unsupported and unplayed; not all vanilla combinations
are audited. Lineages are local, not Steam Cloud data. Host migration is unsupported.
Avoid force-closing during the result transition; full crash-transaction recovery
is still tracked in issue #12.

Use `TheArchitect-v1.0.1.zip` for manual installation, not GitHub's source archives.
Extract its `TheArchitect` folder into the game's `mods` folder with the game
closed. See `INSTALL.md` for details and `SHA256SUMS` for file hashes.
